# SCRAPSHIFT asset shortlist

Research date: 2026-10-02. Read-only research; no assets acquired, purchased, imported, or added to the repository. Product pages were retrieved successfully in USD (`AC_CURR=USD`) and their displayed prices/compatibility tables checked. Contents below come from the official store's public search metadata. Actual Unity 6000.3.25f1 import, shader behavior, appearance, and performance remain unverified.

## Recommendation

Start with Batya Studios' **free Low poly junkyard models pack**: its rusty 256px textures, appliances and pallets fit the retro yard unusually well. Test a few materials in URP before importing everything; this is an old package with custom mobile shaders. Add the free Pretorius Lab tools/drums/pallets only where the first pack lacks them. For a paid supplement, **Scrapyard Props ($9.99)** has the clearest Unity 6 URP evidence and recognizably rusty mechanical parts. Reserve the remaining budget until those previews have been assessed together in-game.

The three paid candidates total **$29.98 before tax**; buying all three is not recommended automatically. The tyre candidate needs material conversion but has a small atlas and low-triangle LODs. Neither a product title nor its pipeline table guarantees compatibility with this project's exact Unity/URP versions.

## Paid candidates within the $50 ceiling

1. **[Scrapyard Props](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/scrapyard-props-230508) — CoolWorks Studio — $9.99 displayed USD.** 12 meshes/prefabs: truck axle/cabin/engine/frame, canister, dashboard frame, rod, shed, steering wheel, fuel tank and wheel/disc; 44 4096px texture files, 12 URP PBR materials and 12 HDRP materials. **URP compatibility explicitly listed at Unity 6000.0.36f1**; HDRP at 6000.0.34f1 and Built-in at 2021.3.31f1. Rusty realistic machinery complements the yard. Triangle counts are mixed: rod 804, steering wheel 1,396, wheel/disc 4,792 + 4,566, engine 36,303, cabin 29,109, shed 33,662. Use selected props, downscale textures, and assess repeated-instance draw calls; no LOD guarantee found. Best paid first choice.

2. **[Scrapyard Environment — Modular Industrial Junkyard (PBR, URP/HDRP Ready)](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/environments/scrapyard-environment-modular-industrial-junkyard-pbr-urp-hdrp-r-377622) — Enviable Game Studios — $15 displayed USD.** Barrels, containers, crates, dumpster, pipes, shed, tree and modular warehouses. Listed examples: crate 48 triangles, dumpster 628, warehouse 1,072/2,366, pipes 5,600/6,300; 8 texture files and 9 materials in the concrete technical list. Pipeline table says all three compatible at 2022.3.62f2. **Caution:** description says created as HDRP, URP file included, and requires changing Mobile Unlit to URP/Lit. It also gives contradictory texture/LOD summaries (specific 2048px vs generic 1K–4K; “LOD if applicable”). Do not assume seamless PBR/LOD readiness from its title. Potential inexpensive background kit, inspect screenshots/content before buying.

3. **[Car Wheel Tire Tyre Rim — Props For Low Poly Mobile Environment](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/car-wheel-tire-tyre-rim-props-for-low-poly-mobile-environment-273839) — TIBITEK GAMES — $4.99 displayed USD.** Two models (tyre/rim), three prefabs, four LODs. Tyre **400/160/80/40 triangles**, rim **508/252/28/12 triangles**. One 2048px tyre atlas and one 256px rim atlas; 4.9MB package. Assemble original small stacks from these shared meshes/materials rather than purchasing expensive heaps. Pipeline table says Built-in compatible, **URP/HDRP not compatible**, at Unity 2022.3.16f1; budget supported URP material conversion and downscale tyre texture. Texture assets include images from **Textures.com**, with a specific warning that images may not be redistributed by default. Keep source texture/package files private; review the included license. Excellent mesh-budget fit, but less plug-and-play than CoolWorks.

**Rejected heavy alternative:** [Tire Pile Junk](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/3d/props/tire-pile-junk-379228) by 32cm was also checked: $6.99 displayed USD, pipeline table all compatible at Unity 2021.3.45f2, but some heaps reach **1 million and 2.5 million triangles**, with another 580,000. Its three advertised LODs have no confirmed per-LOD counts. Avoid as the default retro clutter purchase.

## Free external alternatives and licensing distinction

- **Poly Haven (CC0):** official public model catalogue confirms [Old Tyre](https://polyhaven.com/a/old_tyre) by MP, [Barrel 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/Barrel_02) by Jorge Camacho, [Metal Toolbox](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_toolbox) by Mateusz Sadek, and [Metal Tool Chest](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_tool_chest) modeled/textured by John Hutcheson, rigged by Yann Kervran. These are realistic mesh/texture sources, not verified Unity prefabs; budget mesh simplification/LOD, material setup and roughness-to-smoothness conversion. Triangle counts and exact URP conversion were not checked. [Official license](https://polyhaven.com/license) confirms commercial use, modification and redistribution, no required attribution. Assets are CC0; website example renders/logos are separately protected. No asset files downloaded.
- **[Kenney Survival Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/survival-kit):** current page confirms 80 3D files and **CC0**, free download without compulsory donation. Useful low-poly support props, but nature/survival styling and clean flat surfaces are less directly suited to scrapyard realism. The page does not enumerate a confirmed tyre/drum/appliance set; do not buy/import assuming those contents. No engine-specific URP assurance.

**Unity Store license/account prerequisites:** every linked Store page displays Standard Unity Asset Store EULA; paid pages offer Single Entity/Multi Entity and display “Extension Asset” as the license-type label. Review the actual license/seat entitlement when acquiring, including free packs; “free” does not mean CC0. User needs their Unity account and My Assets/Package Manager access; paid items require their checkout. [Unity Store terms/EULA](https://unity.com/legal/as-terms). Store art may be embedded in a licensed game, but raw licensed package sources must not be made publicly downloadable or committed to a public repository. Keep vendor art out of public Git unless its actual license permits redistribution. Preserve this game's original procedural fallback.
